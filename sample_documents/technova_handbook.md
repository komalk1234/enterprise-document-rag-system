# TechNova Solutions – Employee Handbook

*Version 2.0 | Human Resources Department | Effective: January 2025*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to TechNova Solutions. This Employee Handbook outlines the policies, procedures and guidelines that govern employment at TechNova Solutions. All employees are expected to read and comply with the policies described in this document.

TechNova Solutions is committed to providing a professional, inclusive, and high-performance work environment. This handbook is your primary reference for understanding your rights and responsibilities as an employee.

---

## 1. Company Overview

**Company Name:** TechNova Solutions  
**Industry:** Information Technology and Enterprise Software  
**Mission:** To deliver innovative technology solutions that empower businesses worldwide.  
**Values:** Integrity, Innovation, Collaboration, Excellence.

---

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

### 2.1 Standard Working Hours

Employees are required to work standard office hours of **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday**. This represents a total of 45 working hours per week, including a one-hour lunch break.

### 2.2 Punctuality

All employees are expected to report to work on time. Recurring tardiness may be addressed through the performance management process.

### 2.3 Flexible Arrangements

Flexible start and end times may be available for certain roles, subject to manager approval and business needs. Any flexibility arrangements must be documented and approved in writing.

---

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Every confirmed employee is entitled to **20 paid annual leave days** per calendar year. Annual leave accumulates from the first day of employment. Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance and are subject to manager approval.

Unused leave of up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Unused leave exceeding this limit will lapse at the end of the year.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **10 paid sick leave days** per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

### 3.3 Casual Leave

Each employee is entitled to 5 casual leave days per year for personal or family matters. Casual leave must be applied for in advance where possible.

### 3.4 Maternity / Paternity Leave

- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
- Paternity Leave: 8 working days of paid paternity leave.

---

## 4. Work From Home Policy

### 4.1 Remote Work Eligibility

TechNova Solutions supports flexible working arrangements. **Employees may work remotely up to two days per week** with prior manager approval. Remote work arrangements must not negatively impact team collaboration or project delivery.

### 4.2 Home Office Requirements

Employees working from home are expected to:
- Maintain a professional workspace.
- Be available during core working hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
- Attend all mandatory meetings via video conference.
- Ensure a stable internet connection.

### 4.3 Equipment

The company will provide a laptop and necessary software for remote work. Employees are responsible for the safety of company equipment at all times.

---

## 5. Probation Period

### 5.1 Duration

All new employees are subject to a **six-month probation period** starting from their date of joining. During this period, the company evaluates the employee's performance, conduct, and cultural fit.

### 5.2 Performance Review

A formal performance review will be conducted at the end of the probation period. Successful completion of probation leads to confirmation of employment.

### 5.3 Leave During Probation

Employees on probation are not entitled to annual or casual leave during the probation period, except for sick leave with valid medical documentation.

---

## 6. Notice Period and Resignation

### 6.1 Notice Period

The **notice period for all employees is 60 days**. This applies to both voluntary resignation and company-initiated termination, except in cases of gross misconduct.

### 6.2 Resignation Process

Employees wishing to resign must submit a written resignation letter to their immediate manager and the HR department. The notice period begins from the date the resignation letter is formally accepted.

### 6.3 Garden Leave

The company reserves the right to place employees on garden leave during their notice period, during which they will receive their regular salary but will not be required to attend work.

---

## 7. Compensation and Payroll

### 7.1 Salary

Employee salaries are reviewed annually based on performance, market benchmarks, and company financial performance.

### 7.2 Payroll Schedule

Salaries are processed and credited on the last working day of each month.

### 7.3 Bonuses

Annual performance bonuses may be awarded based on individual and company performance. Bonus eligibility requires the employee to have completed at least 6 months of confirmed service.

---

## 8. Health Insurance and Benefits

### 8.1 Health Insurance Coverage

TechNova Solutions provides comprehensive health insurance coverage to **all confirmed employees and their immediate family members**. Immediate family members include the employee's spouse and up to two dependent children.

### 8.2 Coverage Details

The health insurance plan covers:
- Hospitalization and surgical expenses.
- Outpatient consultations.
- Diagnostic tests and laboratory services.
- Prescription medications.
- Dental and vision care (limited coverage).

### 8.3 Insurance Provider

Details of the insurance provider and claim procedures are provided to employees upon confirmation of employment.

---

## 9. Code of Conduct and Professional Standards

### 9.1 Professional Behaviour

All employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully in all interactions with colleagues, clients, and partners.

### 9.2 Dress Code

Business casual attire is expected in the office. Formal attire may be required for client meetings and official events.

### 9.3 Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to the HR department. Engaging in activities that compete with TechNova Solutions without prior written consent is strictly prohibited.

---

## 10. Information Security Policy

### 10.1 Confidentiality

All employees must maintain strict confidentiality of company information, client data, and internal processes. **Employees must not share company passwords or confidential information** with any unauthorized person, inside or outside the organization.

### 10.2 Password Policy

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
- Passwords must include a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Employees must not reuse the last 5 passwords.
- Passwords must never be written down or shared with colleagues.

### 10.3 Device Security

All company devices must have approved antivirus software installed and active. Employees must lock their workstations when leaving their desk. Unauthorized installation of software is prohibited.

### 10.4 Data Handling

Employee and client data must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Sensitive data must not be stored on personal devices or unauthorized cloud storage services.

---

## 11. Grievance and Redressal

Employees who have a grievance or complaint should first raise it verbally or in writing with their immediate manager. If the matter is not resolved within 7 working days, the employee may escalate the complaint to the Human Resources department.

All grievances will be handled confidentially and impartially.

---

## 12. Disciplinary Procedure

Disciplinary action may be taken in cases of:
- Repeated violations of company policies.
- Misconduct, insubordination, or unprofessional behaviour.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Dishonesty or fraud.

Disciplinary actions may include verbal warning, written warning, suspension, or termination, depending on the severity of the offence.

---

## 13. Equal Opportunity Employment

TechNova Solutions is an equal opportunity employer. All employment decisions are made without regard to race, religion, gender, age, disability, nationality, or any other protected characteristic.

Harassment or discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

---

## 14. Training and Development

TechNova Solutions invests in the professional growth of its employees. Employees may apply for training programs, certifications, and workshops relevant to their role. Approved training costs may be reimbursed as per the company's training policy.

---

## 15. Amendments

TechNova Solutions reserves the right to modify, update, or replace any policy in this handbook. Employees will be notified of significant changes through official communication channels.
